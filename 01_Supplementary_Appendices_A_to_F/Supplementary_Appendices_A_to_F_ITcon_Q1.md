## A.1 Architectural Rationale: Decoupling Geometric and Operational Information Across Three Autonomous Layers

In brownfield and legacy road, railway, port, and airport infrastructure managed by public transportation agencies—such as Colombia's *Agencia Nacional de Infraestructura* (ANI) and *Instituto Nacional de Vías* (INVIAS)—semantic interoperability fails when domain workflows attempt to embed dynamic operational, financial, and contractual state directly inside monolithic geometric Building Information Modeling (BIM) or Geographic Information System (GIS) files. Geometric representations evolve at survey or design cadence, whereas toll traffic series, pavement structural fatigue measured in equivalent single axle load (`ESAL`), unit price analysis (`APU`) schedules, and concession governance records mutate continuously at transactional cadence.

Following the architectural paradigm articulated for legacy infrastructure interoperability, SITI enforces a strict separation between **Language A** (physical-spatial and functional classification of geographic zones, modal infrastructure complexes, functional spaces, constructive systems, and physical elements across Tables `A10` through `A50`) and **Language B** (contractual management, institutional actors, resources/rolling stock, work breakdown `APU` activities, and technical/regulatory parameters across Tables `B10` through `B50`). Rather than mutating source files or duplicating geometry inside operational records, SITI distributes authority across three orthogonal, non-redundant computational substrates and reconstructs cross-domain relationships dynamically at query time:

1. **Layer 1 (`C1` — Normative Semantic Substrate: OWL 2 DL, SKOS, W3C SHACL, and ISO 16739-1:2024):** Establishes the canonical taxonomy (`16,230` active unique classes across 10 authoritative CSV tables and 7 hierarchical levels), description logic TBox axioms (`438,194` RDF triples, `157` object properties under 3 organizing superproperties, and `814` existential/universal restrictions), multilingual terminology (`2,892` reviewed SKOS expressions across 5 languages), closed-world validation shapes (`104` W3C SHACL node shapes), and standardized property/quantity definitions (`849` ISO 16739-1:2024 `Pset` and `Qto` templates).
2. **Layer 2 (`C2` — Topological Articulation Substrate: Labeled Property Graph in openCypher):** Materializes the structural and operational coupling topology as a directed acyclic taxonomic hierarchy (`16,232` deterministic `UUID` version 5 nodes and `16,228` `:SUBCLASE_DE` edges) augmented with `793` deduplicated inter-tabular and transversal edges (`A10->A50`, `A<->B`, and `Bx<->By` across `43` `openCypher` edge types) and `13` canonical assembly recipes (`REC-A-01..07` and `REC-B-01..06`). Layer 2 enforces zero intra-tabular meronymy (`0` edges where source and target share the same table) and isolates localized dependency neighborhoods via exact Markov Blankets $\partial\mathcal{M}(v)$.
3. **Layer 3 (`C3` — Columnar Analytical and Bimodal Spatial Lakehouse in Apache Parquet and DuckDB):** Stores immutable empirical observations, bimodal spatial geometries (`EPSG:9377` / `EPSG:4326` Well-Known Binary polygons/lines coupled with bimodal 64-bit EPSG:9377 anchor + 32-bit local ENU offset $4\times 4$ affine transformation matrices), contractual telemetry, toll collections, and decoupled `APU` cost structures across `2,664` active Apache Parquet datasets (`3,880,617` rows; `569,916,655` bytes).

## D.1 Formal Mathematical Definition of the Third Dimension of the Semantic Interoperability Index ($\Phi_{\text{eff}}$)

To test and quantify the core architectural hypothesis evaluated in this study—that for legacy infrastructure, semantic interoperability is achieved more effectively by separating geometric information from operational information and reconstructing relationships dynamically at query time—we define the third dimension of the Semantic Interoperability Index ($\Phi_{\text{eff}}$) across three complementary operational sub-metrics:

1. **Query-Time Relationship Reconstruction Efficiency ($\Phi_{\text{eff}}^{\text{(Query-Time)}}$):**
   $$\Phi_{\text{eff}}^{\text{(Query-Time)}}(s) = \left(1 - \frac{T_{\text{SITI}, s}}{T_{\text{Baseline}}}\right) \times 100\%, \qquad T_{\text{SITI}, s} = t_{\text{C1 (SKOS/OWL/SHACL)}} + t_{\text{C2 (LPG O(1) + Markov Blanket)}} + t_{\text{C3 (DuckDB Vectorized SIMD Parquet)}}$$
   evaluated across a canonical benchmark of **$N = 50$ real lifecycle engineering and asset-management queries** spanning topological hop depths $k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$ and all **13 formal intertabular assembly recipes (`REC-A-01..07`, `REC-B-01..06`)**, comparing the three-layer SITI pipeline (`M3`) against coupled baselines (`M1`, `M2`) and controlled engine/LBD ablations (`M1b`, `M2b`, `M2c`, detailed in Section D.4B):
   - **`M1` (`Monolithic RDF/Triple-Store Traversal`):** evaluation of $k$-hop Basic Graph Pattern (BGP) `(s, p, o)` triple joins over the `438,194` TBox triples of `capa1_siti_ontology.ttl` plus materialized ABox instance triples without Layer 2 O(1) pointer adjacency or Layer 3 columnar decoupling.
   - **`M2` (`Traditional Row-Oriented Relational SQL / Coupled Wide Table & Multi-Way B-Tree JOINs`):** execution over row-oriented relational tables (`sqlite3` B-Tree storage with coupled geometry payloads and $k$-way relational `JOIN`s).
   - **`M3` (`SITI Three-Layer Query-Time Reconstruction`):** decoupled execution `C1 -> C2 -> C3` with *Late Spatial Materialization*, touching `assets_spatial_instances_ani.parquet` (`226,322` WKB/GeoJSON geometries in `EPSG:9377`) or `assets_spatial_instances.parquet` (`5,011` bimodal 64-bit EPSG:9377 anchor + 32-bit local ENU offset instances) strictly when the query requests spatial coordinates.
2. **Topological Causal Isolation via Pearl's Markov Blanket ($\Phi_{\text{eff}}^{\text{(Markov-Isolation)}}$):**
   $$\Phi_{\text{eff}}^{\text{(Markov-Isolation)}}(v) = \left(1 - \frac{|\partial\mathcal{M}(v)| + 1}{|\mathcal{V}_{C2}|}\right) \times 100\%, \qquad \partial\mathcal{M}(v) = \text{Pa}(v) \cup \text{Ch}(v) \cup \text{Sp}(v)$$
   evaluated across all **`316` intertabular anchor nodes** and all **`16,232` total concepts** in Layer 2 (`17,021` total edges: `16,228` `SUBCLASE_DE` + `793` deduplicated intertabular edges, `0` intratabular meronymy edges), achieving a mean search-space reduction of **`99.9152%`** across the 316 anchors (`99.9862%` across all 16,232 nodes).
3. **Shift-Left Source Authoring Fidelity ($\Phi_{\text{eff}}^{\text{(Shift-Left-Kits)}}$):**
   Physical cryptographic audit of the **9 Shift-Left Authoring Kits** (`5` OpenBIM kits in `Kits_BIM/` and `4` GIS kits in `Kits_GIS/`), demonstrating how pre-tagged authoring templates prevent at the source the `49` `unsupported` (`26` mute IFCs, `21` unstructured TXTs, `1` proprietary binary RVT, `1` malformed CSV) and `174` `partial` cold-ingestion receipts identified in Wave 2.

## E.1 Architectural Rationale for Physical Geometry-versus-Operation Decoupling

Coupled monolithic geometry-and-telemetry architectures that load full 3D/2D coordinate meshes into memory alongside operational attributes (such as synthetic 4.35 GB monolithic workloads evaluated on 1 TB RAM servers) obscure the physical mechanism through which legacy civil infrastructure achieves scalable interoperability on commodity hardware. This appendix provides reproducible physical measurements over the audited SITI Layer 3 production lakehouse (`telemetry_parquet_store/`), testing the first pillar of the post-coordinated interoperability hypothesis:

> *For legacy infrastructure, semantic interoperability is achieved more effectively by separating geometric information from operational information and reconstructing relationships dynamically at query time.*

Across Colombia's national concessioned road, railway, and airport network (`N = 226,324` inventoried assets across `21` functional asset layers), separating geometric payloads from operational telemetry yields three mathematically governed advantages:

1. **Physical Storage and Bandwidth Asymmetry (`24.67×` on Disk, `32.11×` in Uncompressed RAM):**
   - The national spatial container `telemetry_parquet_store/assets_spatial_instances_ani.parquet` (`226,322` geometry instances carrying `MAGNA-SIRGAS EPSG:9377` and `WGS84 EPSG:4326` `GeoJSON`/coordinate payloads totaling `23,263,741` 2D vertices) occupies **`455,875,112` bytes (`455.88 MB`)** on disk (reported as `454.32 MB` in the Wave 1 nominal pre-index snapshot) and expands to **`1,394,990,868` bytes (`1,394.99 MB` / `1.395 GB`)** in uncompressed Arrow/Parquet memory, of which **`98.82%`** consists strictly of coordinate buffers.
   - Conversely, the decoupled operational inventory `telemetry_parquet_store/siti_ani_activos_principales.parquet` (`226,324` rows across `30` semantic, contractual, and condition columns—`21` core operational attributes plus `9` forensic lineage columns) occupies only **`18,480,056` bytes (`18.48 MB`)** on disk (`19.21 MB` nominal) and **`43,441,449` bytes (`43.44 MB`)** uncompressed—a **`24.67×` reduction in physical disk mass** (`23.65×` nominal) and a **`2,071.36×` (`99.95%`) I/O reduction** when projecting operational columns in `SoA` (`0.220 MB`). Similarly, national OETR toll telemetry (`peajes_ani_detalles_global.parquet` + `peajes_ani_encabezados_global.parquet`) occupies `4.48 MB`, and the decoupled INVIAS unit-price catalog (`siti_apu_invias_desacoplado_ab.parquet`, `526` national items representing `73,640` regional rows across `140` books) occupies `58.55 KB` deduplicated (`3.81 MB` expanded).
2. **Sparsity Collapse via `Unboxed EAV` in `SoA` (`99.90%` $\to$ `0.00%` Structural Null Padding):**
   - Coupling SITI Language B's `11,544` operational classes (or its `738` `B50` performance/cost indicator classes and `100` SHACL-governed parametric shapes `PAR_B50_0001..0100`) directly onto `226,324` physical assets creates a `2,617,437,060`-cell sparse matrix (`11,565` columns: `21` dense base + `11,544` `Language B`) with **`99.7147%` total-table `NULL` cells** (`99.8960%` across the `11,544` `Language B` columns; and **`95.6522%` total-table `NULL` cells** [`98.3740%` across `738` `B50` columns] for the `759`-column `B50` wide table). The Layer 3 Unboxed EAV columnar storage engine removes wide-table null bitmaps by storing strongly typed primitive vectors (`val_float64`, `val_int64`, `val_string`, `val_bool`) reconciled via immutable bitemporal windowing (`ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY asset_uuid, attribute_code_b50 ORDER BY valid_from_utc DESC, sys_created_at_utc DESC)`) with **`0` mutable `is_active` flags** and RFC 9562 `UUIDv7` + `SHA-256` cryptographic hash chaining.

## E.5 Geodesic IEEE 754 Quantization Audit (*GPU Vertex Jitter*) Across 226,322 National Assets and 5,011 Topocentric `ENU` / `OpenUSD` Instances (`Table E.4`)
